Eastlake Infill Development Offering

2033 Minor Avenue East, Seattle, Washington 98102
Sixteen units. One remaining site. Decades of family stewardship behind it.

The Brief

Position, Plans, and a Path to Permit

No development conditions. No floodplain. No shoreline overlay. Urban Growth Area confirmed. The site is ready to build.

2033 Minor Avenue East represents one of the last undeveloped multifamily parcels on this block of Eastlake — a neighborhood positioned directly between South Lake Union and the University District, two of Seattle's largest employment anchors.

The property is offered with a substantially complete development package, including architectural and engineering plans, landscape planning, and preliminary permitting documentation prepared through Surround Architecture. Two development schemes are under consideration: a four-story, 16-unit apartment building and a five-story, 16-unit condominium project with below-grade parking. The seller invites highest and best unconditional offers from qualified developers on or before July 1, 2026.
