Eastlake Infill Development Offering

2033 Minor Avenue East, Seattle, Washington 98102
Sixteen units. One remaining site. Decades of family stewardship.

The Brief

Position, Plans, and a Path to Permit

Urban Growth Area confirmed. The site is ready to build.

2033 Minor Avenue East represents one of the last undeveloped multifamily parcels on this block of Eastlake — a neighborhood positioned directly between South Lake Union and the University District, two of Seattle's largest employment anchors.

The property is offered with a substantially complete development package, including architectural and engineering plans, landscape planning, and preliminary permitting documentation prepared through Surround Architecture. Two development schemes are under consideration: a four-story, 16-unit apartment building and a five-story, 16-unit condominium project with plans for below-grade parking for 13 cars.

The condominium scheme includes plans for below-grade parking for 13 cars. Buyers should be aware that the proposed access drive may require SDCI review given its proximity to a protected tree root zone, and approval is not assured. An on-grade parking alternative is available. Buyers are encouraged to independently verify current code interpretations prior to submitting an offer.

The seller invites highest and best unconditional offers from qualified developers on or before July 31, 2026.
