Project Location & Site

Neighborhood Context

Eastlake sits at the geographic midpoint between South Lake Union and the University District, two of Seattle's largest employment anchors, in a neighborhood that functions as a genuine residential community rather than a transit corridor. Minor Avenue East is the quieter, residential spine running parallel to the arterial — walkable, Lake Union-adjacent, and consistently in demand. 2033 is one of the last undeveloped multifamily sites on the block.

Existing Improvements

The parcel contains a 2,464 sq ft wood-frame structure built in 1905, originally constructed as a private residence and subsequently converted to professional office use. The building currently operates as eight private therapist suites under annual leases. The landlord retains the right to terminate existing leases with six months' prior written notice. The existing structure is expected to be demolished as part of redevelopment.
