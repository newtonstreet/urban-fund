The Development Vision

Two Development Paths, Fully Documented

Surround Architecture advanced two distinct development schemes for 2033 Minor Avenue East — a four-story apartment building and a five-story condominium with below-grade parking. Both are designed to the same material and architectural standard: dark charcoal panel facade, cedar vertical fins, large-format glazing, flat roofline. The difference is program, financing structure, and exit strategy. The drawings, floor plates, and planning documentation for both schemes are included in this offering.
